Privacy Policy

1. Who operates the app

ID Photo Maker (package com.joyahan.idphoto) is operated by an individual developer publishing under the Google Play developer name Joya Han. This policy explains how the app and this policy website handle information. Contact liusuiyun713@gmail.com about privacy or your rights.

2. Photos and on-device processing

Photo editing, face positioning and background removal run on your device. Photos are not uploaded to an editing service or sent to advertisers. Face positioning helps compose the photo; it is not a cloud identity-recognition service.

Android's photo picker gives access to selected photos. Older devices may request image access to show local albums. Camera access is requested when you take a photo; only a capture you confirm is used for editing. You can decline or revoke permissions in Android settings, which may limit the corresponding feature.

3. Local files and preferences

Private works and drafts can retain the original input, image layers including transparency, and edit history so you can resume editing. Input files are copied byte for byte and may retain embedded metadata, including location if present in the source. These files use app-private storage designated to exclude Android app backup. Older works may contain only a finished image.

The app also stores local history, preferences, notice versions and confirmation times. Local diagnostic information helps investigate failures; the app does not automatically send these local logs.

4. Copies you export or share

Saving to your gallery, exporting a file or PDF, sharing and printing create or deliver independent copies to destinations you choose without overwriting the source photo. Receiving apps, storage providers and print services handle those copies under their own policies. Your gallery or destination may synchronize them to the cloud. Deleting private app data does not remove those copies; manage them at their destinations.

5. Google configuration and advertising services

After you confirm the app's notice, Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations connect to Google to retrieve advertising settings, including when advertising is disabled. They send a Firebase installation identifier, installation authentication information, the country or region code from your device's locale, language, time zone, platform, operating system, app identifier, app and build version, package name and SDK version information. The locale region code is not a GPS location.

When advertising services run, Google AdMob may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, app and advertising interactions (such as app launches, ad clicks and video views), performance and diagnostic data, and applicable identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and App Set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These SDK connections use TLS encryption in transit.

Google UMP manages applicable regional privacy choices and processes those choices along with app, device and request information needed to request and present consent forms. This version has no account, in-app purchases, subscriptions, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or Meta SDK. AdMob nevertheless performs its own analytics processing. Google's policy explains its handling and retention: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your advertising choices

Confirming this policy does not consent to personalized advertising. Where applicable, make or withdraw choices through Google's consent form or Settings > Ad privacy choices. Declining personalized ads does not stop every network request: configuration, consent management and permitted advertising may still require data processing. You can also review Android's advertising privacy controls. Where law requires consent for processing, that consent must be obtained separately and may be withdrawn.

8. Retention and deletion

Private works and drafts remain until you delete them. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private app data, including local preferences and confirmation records. Keep needed originals and exports first.

Temporary editing, camera and printing files are cleaned during normal workflows; an interruption or crash may delay cleanup. Sharing cache files become eligible for removal after seven days, but cleanup runs when you next share, so files may remain longer. Google retains information stored by its services under the rules applicable to each service; deleting the app does not necessarily delete that information. See also Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
